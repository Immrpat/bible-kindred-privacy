Privacy policy

Bible Kindred for Android
Effective 4 November 2026

Bible Kindred is an offline Bible and story app. The developer does not run an account system and does not upload the notes you write in the app to a Bible Kindred server. This page explains what stays on your phone and what Google may receive when ads, reviews, or Play purchases are used.

Who this covers

The app package is com.biblekindred.app. It is published by the developer using the GitHub account Immrpat. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Information stored on your phone

These items stay in the app’s private storage on the device:

  • A short note you choose to save with a story, and whether you marked that day’s step done.
  • Which story you opened last, so the app can show continue.
  • Whether you finished the introduction.
  • Which locked stories were opened with an ad, so that story stays open on this phone.
  • The time the app last asked you for a Play review, so it does not ask again for several months.
  • A morning reminder for each story you ask to be reminded about. Each reminder is scheduled on the phone. The app asks for notification permission only if you turn a reminder on.

Uninstalling the app deletes this local data. The app does not send these notes to the developer.

Safety screen

The “Not safe” screen shows phone numbers and can open your phone’s dialer. Bible Kindred does not save what you almost typed there, and it does not show an ad on that screen.

Sharing

If you tap share, Android opens the share sheet. The text is sent only to the app you pick. Bible Kindred does not operate that other app.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob.

  • A full-screen interstitial ad can play before a locked story opens.
  • An app-open ad can play when you leave the app and come back.

Google may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with an ad, including approximate location inferred from the IP address. Ads may be personalized according to your Google ad settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalization, in your device settings.

Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. How AdMob uses data is described at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Debug builds request Google’s test ads instead of the live ad units.

Google Play review

After you mark a story step done, the app may ask Google Play to show its review sheet. Google decides whether the sheet appears. The star rating and any review text are handled by Google Play, not sent to a Bible Kindred server. The app only stores, on the phone, the time it last made that request.

Purchases

Bible Kindred does not sell a subscription and does not collect payment details.

Scripture text

The Bible text in the app is the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain. It is stored in the app install. Reading a chapter does not send the chapter to the developer.

How long information is kept

Notes and settings remain on the phone until you delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. A story opened with an ad stays open on that phone. Google keeps advertising and Play data under Google’s own retention rules.

Your choices

  • You can refuse notification permission. Stories still open. A morning reminder will not be posted.
  • You can ignore or dismiss a review request.
  • You can limit ad personalization in your device’s Google settings.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove its local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact
